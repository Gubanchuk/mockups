Image labeling question

Three versions for the phone. On a laptop all three look the same.

1Version 1 · Image + listThe image fits the screen. Under it, one numbered row per cell: tap a row or a cell, then a label. 2Version 2 · MagnifierTap a cell: a sheet opens with that part of the image enlarged and the labels under it. Arrows go to the next cell. 3Version 3 · Full screenTurn the phone: the image fills the screen with the cells at their real size. Tap a cell and pick a label.
