Program rules

Commission
A fixed amount for each HESI Exam Study plan: from $8 for 30 Days to $20 for 36 Months Access. The amount is the same when the student pays with a payment plan.
What counts
HESI Exam Study purchases made within 30 days after a click on your link. If the student clicks another affiliate's link later, the purchase goes to the last link clicked. Card, Afterpay, Affirm, Klarna and PayPal payments all count. HESI A2 MasterClass purchases don't earn a commission.
Hold
Each commission is on hold for 30 days after the purchase. Then it becomes available.
Payouts
We pay your available balance to your PayPal account once it reaches $50.
Refunds
If an order is refunded, its commission is cancelled. If the commission was already paid out, it's deducted from your next payout.
Joining
Anyone can apply. We review every application and email you once it's approved.
