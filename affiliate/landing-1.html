Good to know

Last click counts
If someone clicked several affiliate links before buying, the purchase counts for the last one.
30-day hold
Each commission is on hold for 30 days after the purchase, then it becomes available.
Payouts
Once you have $100 or more available, request a payout in your dashboard. We send it to your PayPal within a few days.
Refunds
If an order is refunded, fully or in part, its commission is cancelled. If it was already paid out, it's deducted from your future payouts.
Not allowed
Purchases through your own link don't earn a commission: this isn't a discount program. Don't spam your link or generate fake clicks, or we may suspend or close your account.
Rate changes
The amount is fixed at the moment of purchase. If a rate changes, the new one applies only to later purchases.
Rates
Every plan has its own fixed commission, and plans paid with Klarna, Afterpay or Affirm count too. You'll see the rates in your dashboard once you're approved.
