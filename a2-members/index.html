A2 member area

Four versions of the main page of the HESI A2 MasterClass member area. At the top of each one: Version switches between the redesign only (what the site already does) and the redesign with new features (first-visit setup, exam settings, the subjects of your exam, practice test scores on Home). Menu puts the site menu on top or on the left, on a computer. Claire AI adds an AI helper that answers from Claire's lessons; AI state shows it writing, unable to answer and out of questions for the day. First visit shows the setup screen a student sees on the first login after buying; the other pages open from the menu of the page itself. Student switches the sample student, Screen switches desktop and phone. New labels mark what the site does not have yet and can be hidden.

Favorites

1Version 1 · Study & ProgressLaid out like exam-prep apps. Study is Home: the next lesson with its video and one button. Lessons lists every subject, Progress holds your numbers, Questions collects Claire's answers. 2Version 2 · Course mapThe whole course as one picture: every lesson is a square in the row of its subject, practice tests close each row. The next lesson sits on top with one button.

More versions

3Version 3 · TutorWhat to do next, in one teal block with one button. With the new features it becomes a short list for today, built from your exam date and pace. Tabs at the bottom on a phone. 4Version 4 · One goalA learning-platform home in the manner of Coursera: one card with your exam, how far along you are and the next lesson. Subjects as plain rows.
