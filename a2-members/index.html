A2 member area

Three versions of the main page of the HESI A2 MasterClass member area. At the top of each one: Version switches between the redesign only (what the site already does) and the redesign with new features (first-visit setup, exam settings, the subjects of your exam, practice test scores on Home). Page opens the other screens, Student switches the sample student, Screen switches desktop and phone. New labels mark what the site does not have yet and can be hidden.

1Version 1 · TutorWhat to do next, in one teal block with one button. With the new features it becomes a short list for today, built from your exam date and pace. Tabs at the bottom on a phone. 2Version 2 · One goalA learning-platform home in the manner of Coursera: one card with your exam, how far along you are and the next lesson. Subjects as plain rows. 3Version 3 · My examClaire's plan on a blue band with the exam details in one line, then one big next step and your progress beside it. Subjects below.
