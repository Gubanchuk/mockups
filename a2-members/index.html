A2 member area

Three versions of the main page of the HESI A2 MasterClass member area. At the top of each one you can switch the student (just started, in progress, all lessons done) and the screen (desktop, phone). The New tag marks what the site does not have yet.

1Version 1 · My examThe exam is the frame: days left, the score your school needs and only the subjects on your exam. Claire's plan is a band on top, folded to one line once you have started. 2Version 2 · TutorA plan for today made from your subjects, the diagnostic quiz and your exam date, with a tab bar at the bottom on a phone. Ask Claire is the one AI feature: a helper that answers from Claire's lessons. 3Version 3 · Acceptance letterThe road into your nursing school, in the look of the YBG site: what your school asks for, and where you stand in each subject against it.
