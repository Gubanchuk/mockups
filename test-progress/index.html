Progress in a test

Three ways to show the percentage above the question in a HESI or A2 test. The rest of the page is as on the site. Question switches between the first, a middle and the last question, Screen between desktop and phone.

1Version 1 · Readable markerThe marker still follows the bar, now as a blue label with the percentage in normal size. At the start and at the end of a test it stays inside the bar. 2Version 2 · Bar with the percentage"Question 21 of 55" on the left, the percentage on the right, a thicker bar under them. The bar takes the place of the grey line above the question. 3Version 3 · Next to the countThe percentage goes into the line with the count: "21 of 55 · 38%". The marker above the bar is gone.
